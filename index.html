Where your data actually lives

On your device
The ledger is a database on your phone. SyncLedger keeps working with the network off, and your entries are there when you come back.
In your own Google Drive
Cloud sync writes to the Google account you connect. Altusvay runs no server that holds your records.
Protected before it leaves
Shared ledger payloads are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded.
Yours to take with you
Export everything to JSON or CSV whenever you want. The export is the same data the app reads.

What it costs

Free

Your personal ledger is free forever, with no limit on entries. Add one more ledger of your own with up to 400 entries at no cost. Joining a ledger that someone else owns is always free, however many you join.

Unlimited ledgers and unlimited entries. Monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. The current price is shown in Google Play before you buy.

Reaching a limit never deletes, hides or locks anything you have already recorded. Everything stays readable and exportable.
