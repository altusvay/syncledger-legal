Atlas Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Atlas is a personal AI assistant operated by Altusvay. This Privacy Policy explains what information Atlas handles when it is connected to a Google Account, why it is handled, what is shared, and how to stop it.

1. Who this policy covers

Atlas is a single-operator tool: it runs on one person's own computer and acts on that same person's own Google Account. There is no public sign-up, no other users, and no service that stores data on behalf of third parties. "You" below means that operator.

2. Google user data Atlas accesses

Atlas accesses Google user data only after you grant it through Google's consent screen, and only under the scopes you approve:

Google serviceWhat is accessed
GmailRead and search your messages, read and modify labels, and create or send mail at your request.
CalendarRead your events and calendars, and create or delete events at your request.
DriveRead, create, upload, download, share, and delete files and folders in your Drive.
Docs and SheetsRead and edit your documents and spreadsheets, including creating new ones.
ContactsRead your contacts, for lookups by name.

Atlas does not access the Google data of any other person, and it does not read your data in the background for its own purposes. It acts when you ask it to act.

3. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to carry out the request you made of the assistant. In practice that means:

  • finding, reading, summarising, drafting, or sending mail you asked about;
  • reading and updating your calendar;
  • reading and writing the files, documents, and spreadsheets you asked it to work with; and
  • looking up a contact you asked about.

Google user data is not used for advertising, is not used to train artificial-intelligence models, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles of you or anyone else.

4. Limited Use disclosure

Atlas's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features of the assistant, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition, and is never used for advertising or for training machine-learning models.

5. Where your information is stored

Atlas has no central server and no database of its own. Your Google user data lives in your Google Account and is read from it on demand. Working copies, logs, and your Google authorization credentials are stored on your own computer, on the machine where the assistant runs, and are readable only by your own user account on that machine.

6. What is shared with others

  • Google. Atlas talks to Google APIs directly, using your authorization. Google's own privacy policy governs Google's processing.
  • AI model providers. To generate a reply, the content involved in your request — for example the text of an email you asked it to summarise, or a document you asked it to edit — is sent to the artificial-intelligence model provider configured in the assistant. It is sent to answer your request, and no more of it is sent than the request needs. These providers process that content under their own terms; they are not given your Google password, and they are not authorized to use the content for advertising, and Atlas does not permit its Google user data to be used to train models. If you would prefer not to send a particular message or document to a model provider, do not ask the assistant to process it.
  • No one else. Atlas does not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with data brokers, advertisers, or any other third party, and does not use it for creditworthiness or lending purposes.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Because Atlas holds no server-side copy, deleting a message, file, or event in Google deletes it for Atlas too. Atlas creates no shadow copy that outlives the original.
  • You can revoke Atlas's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking ends all further access immediately; the stored authorization credentials stop working, and Atlas cannot refresh them.
  • You can delete the credentials and any working copies from your own computer by removing the assistant's credential and cache files on that machine.
  • To ask for deletion of anything Atlas recorded locally, write to the address in section 10 and it will be removed; if you cannot reach it yourself, the request describes which machine and which data, since the data is not held anywhere else.

8. Security

Access is through Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization; Atlas never sees, asks for, or stores your Google password. Credentials are held in files readable only by your own user account on your machine, and communication with Google and with model providers uses TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Children's privacy

Atlas is not directed to children under 13, and it is not made available to them.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Altusvay at atlas.support@altusvay.com.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Atlas's features, its Google integrations, or legal requirements change. The updated version is published on this page with a revised effective date.
