Atlas

Atlas is a personal AI assistant operated by Altusvay. It runs on the operator's own computer and, with the operator's explicit permission, connects to the operator's own Google Account so it can read, summarise, file, and act on that operator's own information.

Privacy policy: https://syncledger.altusvay.com/atlas/privacy-policy.html

Who uses Atlas

Atlas is a single-operator tool. It is used by one person, on that person's own Google Account, on that person's own machine. There is no public sign-up, no multi-tenant service, and the application is not distributed to other users. Every Google permission Atlas holds was granted by that operator through Google's own consent screen, and the operator can withdraw it at any time.

What Atlas does with a Google Account

  • Gmail — read and search the operator's mail, summarise it, prepare and send replies and drafts, and apply labels.
  • Calendar — read upcoming events and create or remove events at the operator's request.
  • Drive — read, create, upload, download, share, and delete files and folders in the operator's own Drive.
  • Docs and Sheets — read and edit the operator's documents and spreadsheets, including creating new ones.
  • Contacts — read the operator's contacts, for lookups by name.

Atlas acts only when the operator asks it to, and only on the operator's own data. It does not access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, or Contacts belonging to anyone else.
